# 🎬 STREAMNEXA
## Subscription Management & Customer Retention Analytics System

### 📌 1. Project Scenario

StreamNexa is a subscription-based digital entertainment company providing online streaming services to customers across different cities in India. The company offers multiple subscription plans, including Basic, Standard, Premium and Family plans.

The company faces challenges in retaining customers, as some subscribers cancel their subscriptions, fail to renew their plans or become inactive over time.

The organization wants to develop a Subscription Management & Customer Retention Analytics System to store, manage and analyse customer, subscription, payment and usage data using a structured SQL database.

The system will help the company understand customer behaviour, monitor subscription performance, analyse payment activities and identify factors responsible for customer cancellations.

### 🗄️ 2. Database Tables

#### 👥 1. Customers

The company stores personal and registration information for every customer, including:

- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Gender
- Age
- Email
- Phone
- City
- Registration Date
- Customer Status

A customer can purchase one or more subscription plans over time and can have multiple subscription records.

#### 📦 2. Subscription Plans

The company offers different subscription plans to customers.

For example:
- Basic
- Standard
- Premium
- Family

For each plan, the system stores:

- Plan ID
- Plan Name
- Plan Type
- Plan Price
- Subscription Duration
- Plan Description
- Plan Status

A subscription plan can be associated with multiple customers and subscription records.

#### 🔄 3. Subscriptions

Customers subscribe to different plans depending on their requirements.

For each subscription, the system stores:

- Subscription ID
- Customer ID
- Plan ID
- Start Date
- End Date
- Subscription Status
- Auto-Renewal Status
- Cancellation Date

Subscription statuses include:
- Active
- Cancelled
- Expired
- Paused

A customer can have multiple subscriptions over time, and each subscription is associated with a particular plan.

#### 💳 4. Payments

Customers make payments to purchase or renew their subscriptions.

For every payment, the system stores:

- Payment ID
- Subscription ID
- Payment Date
- Payment Amount
- Payment Method
- Payment Status
- Transaction Reference

Payment statuses include:
- Successful
- Failed
- Pending
- Refunded

A subscription can have multiple payment records, allowing analysis of payment history and failed transactions.

#### 📱 5. Usage Activity

The company records customer activity to understand how frequently customers use its services.

For each activity record, the system stores:

- Activity ID
- Customer ID
- Activity Date
- Login Count
- Usage Duration
- Last Active Date
- Device Type

This information helps analyse customer engagement and identify customers with declining usage activity.

#### ❌ 6. Churn Records

The company maintains information about customers who discontinue their subscriptions.

For each churn record, the system stores:

- Churn ID
- Subscription ID
- Customer ID
- Cancellation Date
- Cancellation Reason
- Feedback
- Refund Status

Common cancellation reasons include:
- High Subscription Price
- Lack of Usage
- Better Alternatives
- Poor Service Experience
- Financial Reasons

Churn records help the company understand why customers leave and identify possible improvements in customer retention.

### 📊 3. SQL Business Analysis Question Bank

The following questions will help analyse subscription performance, customer behaviour, churn patterns and revenue.

👥 1. Customer Analysis
1. How many total customers are registered on the platform?
2. How many customers are Active vs Inactive?
3. What is the gender distribution of customers?
4. What is the average age of customers?
5. How many new customers registered in each year?
6. How many customers registered in each month?
7. How many customers are registered in each city?
   
📋 2. Subscription Plan Analysis
1. How many subscription plans are available under each plan type?
2. What is the average price of each subscription plan type?
3. Which are the top 10 most expensive subscription plans?
4. Which subscription plans are available for free?
5. How many subscription plans are available for each duration?
6. How much successful payment revenue is generated by each plan type?
   
🔄 3. Subscription Analysis
1. How many total subscriptions are recorded in the system?
2. What is the distribution of Active, Cancelled, Expired and Paused subscriptions?
3. What are the subscription details of each customer, including their plan name?
4. How many subscriptions are registered under each plan?
5. Which customers have more than one subscription?
6. How many subscriptions have Auto Renewal enabled vs disabled?
7. How many subscriptions were cancelled in each year?
   
💳 4. Payment Analysis
1. How many payments are Successful, Failed, Pending and Refunded?
2. What is the average payment amount for each payment method?
3. How many payments were made using each payment method?
4. Which payments have Failed status?
5. Which subscriptions have received more than one payment?
6. What is the total payment amount for each payment status?
   
📱 5. Customer Usage Analysis
1. What is the total number of logins and usage duration recorded?
2. What is the average usage duration for each device type?
3. How many customers use each device type?
4. Who are the top 10 customers based on total usage duration?
5. Which customers have fewer than 4 logins?
6. What is the total usage duration of customers in each city?
   
📉 6. Churn Analysis
1. How many customers cancelled their subscriptions for each cancellation reason?
2. How many customer churn records were recorded in each month?
3. What are the customer names and feedback details of customers who cancelled?
4. Which cities have the highest number of churned customers?
5. Which subscription plan types have the highest number of churn records?
6. Which customers cancelled due to high subscription prices?
   
🔁 7. Customer Retention Analysis
1. Which customers have subscribed more than once?
2. How many subscriptions have Auto Renewal enabled and are currently Active?
3. How many customers have enabled Auto Renewal?
4. Which customers have renewed their subscriptions?
5. What percentage of subscriptions have Auto Renewal enabled?
   
🎯 8. Customer Segmentation
1. How many customers belong to each age group?
2. How many customers belong to each usage category?
3. How many customers belong to each subscription duration category?
4. Which customers belong to the high-value customer segment?
5. How many customers belong to each subscription plan type?
6. Which customers have low usage but an Active subscription?

💡 9. Customer Insights
1. Which customers are Active but have not been recently active on the platform?
2. Which age group has the highest average usage duration?
3. Which city has the highest average customer usage?
4. Which gender has the highest average subscription spending?
5. Which customers have high login activity but low subscription spending?
   
📊 10. Revenue & Business Performance
1. What is the total revenue generated from successful payments?
2. What is the total successful payment amount received for each subscription?
3. What is the monthly revenue generated from successful payments?
4. Which customers are associated with the highest payment amounts?
5. What is the yearly revenue generated from successful payments?
6. What is the percentage of registered customers who have churn records?
  
  ### 🎓 Project Conclusion

The Subscription Churn & Customer Retention Analytics System provides a comprehensive view of customer subscriptions, payment activities, usage behaviour and cancellation patterns.

The analysis helps the company understand customer behaviour, identify subscription plans with higher churn rates, monitor payment performance and evaluate customer retention.

Customer usage and churn analysis can help identify customers who may be at risk of discontinuing their subscriptions. Revenue analysis helps the organization understand the financial impact of cancellations and subscription renewals.

Overall, this SQL project demonstrates how subscription data can be organized, analysed and transformed into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making and improve customer retention.
